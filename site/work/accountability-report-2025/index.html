Accountability Report 2025

The second edition: bolder color, expressive type and data focused on the territory.

Discipline
Editorial design
Client
Cali City Council
Year
2026
Role
Newspaper design and layout
Tools

View publication

Brief

Present Edison Giraldo’s 2025 progress: downtown revitalization, local business support, community initiatives, public space improvements and political oversight.

Approach

Expressive typography, vibrant color, photography and key data, organized so institutional results read as a clear, approachable and territory focused publication.

Result

Together with the 2024 edition, about 2,000 copies distributed in downtown Cali.
